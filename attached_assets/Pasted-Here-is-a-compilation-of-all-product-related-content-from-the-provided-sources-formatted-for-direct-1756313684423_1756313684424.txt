## Precision Planting Products

This section provides detailed information on Precision Planting products, drawn directly from the provided sources, to help you understand their features, benefits, and research-backed performance.

### 20/20 & 20/20 SeedSense

The 20/20 system, also known as 20/20 SeedSense, is a versatile monitor and control hub designed to enhance planting decisions and equipment performance.

**Key Features & Capabilities:**
*   **Real-Time Monitoring:** Serves as a monitor to precisely collect spatial data and visualize performance and field conditions in real-time, focusing on seed-by-seed, plant-by-plant, and drop-by-drop information. It monitors all aspects of planter performance and its interaction with the soil.
*   **Advanced Agronomic Picture:** Provides the most advanced agronomic view to optimize planting, harvest, and application decisions.
*   **Comprehensive Data Collection:** Tracks crucial parameters such as population, singulation, spacing, ground contact, downforce margin, soil moisture, and soil temperature.
*   **Equipment Control & Automation:** Powers advanced systems for controlling population, downforce, liquid or solid application, multi-hybrid planting, and high-speed planting. It processes real-time data to control equipment during operation, optimizing results.
*   **Intuitive Interface:** Features an intuitive interface with customizable display configurations and real-time feedback.
*   **Data Hub:** The Gen 3 20|20 acts as the central hub for agronomic data and performance metrics from various Precision Planting products.
*   **Seamless Data Transfer:** Automatically pushes data to the Panorama app and website via Wi-Fi for viewing, sharing, and analysis outside the cab.

**Benefits:**
*   **Informed Decision-Making:** Provides precise data for making correct decisions at every step of the production cycle, from planting to equipment purchase.
*   **Maximized Productivity & Yield:** Adjusts equipment to achieve maximum productivity and optimizes every pass.
*   **Confidence in Operation:** Eliminates guesswork and provides confidence in planter performance throughout the season.
*   **Scalable Solutions:** Control systems for seed placement, downforce, fertilizer rate, and faster, accurate planting can be added simultaneously or over time.

### AirForce

AirForce is a downforce control system designed to manage variability in the field and automatically adjust planter downforce needs.

**Key Features & Capabilities:**
*   **Automatic Downforce Adjustment:** Measures field variability and automatically adjusts downforce in response to changing field environments.
*   **Consistent Ground Contact:** Uses downforce and lift bags to provide the correct amount of pressure across the planter, ensuring consistent ground contact without excess gauge wheel weight, even as soil density or seed hopper weight changes.
*   **Enhanced Precision:** Works with the Precision Planting 20/20 SeedSense to offer **infinite downforce settings per field**, rather than a single setting, to maintain yield across varying environments.

**Benefits:**
*   **Prevents Yield Loss:** Addresses the issue of yield loss caused by too much or too little downforce from a single setting across the field.
*   **Optimal Crop Stand:** Crucial for achieving consistent depth, even emergence, and a flourishing root system.

### Clarity

Clarity offers high-definition blockage monitoring for air seeders, box drills, strip-till bars, and dry fertilizer applicators.

**Key Features & Capabilities:**
*   **High-Definition Visibility:** Provides real-time visibility into product flow, surpassing the basic "flowing or not flowing" information of standard blockage systems.
*   **Row-by-Row Display:** The Clarity system displays and maps flow variability and product blockage metrics row-by-row on the Gen 3 20|20 monitor.
*   **Detailed Metrics:**
    *   **Row-by-Row Mapping:** Shows a wide array of colors in high-definition maps that illustrate detailed data for each row.
    *   **Product Magnitude:** Displays the amount of product flowing through each sensor, enabling row-by-row comparisons to quickly spot inconsistencies.
    *   **Product Uniformity:** Provides a numerical percentage value indicating how evenly product is delivered to each row. It uses product magnitude data to show rows that are over-applying or under-applying, with a foot-by-foot snapshot of the equipment average.
    *   **Product Deviation:** Shows how each row's application compares to the average magnitude, displaying the percentage above or below the average for diagnosing performance issues.

**Benefits:**
*   **Quick & Accurate Problem Detection:** Pinpoints blockages or drops in product flow more quickly and accurately.
*   **Operational Confidence:** Allows operators to investigate issues and run equipment with confidence.

### CleanSweep

CleanSweep is a residue management system that allows operators to adjust planter row cleaners from the cab.

**Key Features & Capabilities:**
*   **In-Cab Adjustments:** An air cylinder on each row cleaner, coupled with a cab-mounted controller, allows continuous adjustments to pressure settings.
*   **Flexible Pressure Control:** Operators can apply down pressure or lighten the row cleaners with lift pressure, setting them correctly for any field condition without leaving the cab.

**Benefits:**
*   **Time-Saving & Reduced Frustration:** Eliminates the time-consuming and frustrating manual adjustment of row cleaners.
*   **Improved Emergence Consistency:** Properly adjusted row cleaners remove surface residue, preventing it from being hair-pinned in the furrow, which ensures consistent seed germination and emergence.

**Research Findings:**
*   **2018 PTI Corn Study:** The absence of row cleaners resulted in **-3.3 bushels per acre (Bu/A) yield losses** and **-$11.50/A economic losses** compared to CleanSweep set at 25 PSI of lift. Floating row cleaners also led to -3.8 Bu/A yield losses and -$13.32/A net losses.
*   **2019 PTI Corn Study:** The absence of row cleaners resulted in **-26.1 Bu/A yield losses** and **-$95.79/A economic losses** compared to CleanSweep set at 20 psi of lift. Floating row cleaners showed -2.9 Bu/A yield losses and -$10.64/A net losses. In challenging cloddy conditions, aggressive residue manager settings with CleanSweep were crucial to remove clods, achieve proper planting depths, and ensure seeds were placed into adequate soil moisture, leading to improved stand establishment.

### Conceal

Conceal is a planter fertility attachment designed for efficient and precise fertilizer placement during the planter pass.

**Key Features & Capabilities:**
*   **Cleanly Integrated Design:** The attachment is cleanly tucked into the row-unit, making it less bulky, messy, and high-maintenance than many traditional nitrogen attachments.
*   **Precise Placement:** Places nutrition in the soil **3 inches away from the seed**.
*   **Consistent Depth:** Uses a knife concealed within a groove in the gauge wheel to ensure consistent fertilizer depth.
*   **Protected Nutrients:** Fertilizer is sealed within the soil profile, preventing potential volatilization losses often seen with surface-applied nitrogen.

**Benefits:**
*   **Improved Plant Availability:** Applying fertilizer with the planter enhances placement accuracy and plant availability of nitrogen and other nutrients.
*   **Reduced Nutrient Loss:** Protects fertilizer from volatilization and loss compared to traditional broadcast or fall applications.
*   **Unaltered Planter Performance:** The concealed knife design does not affect other aspects of planter performance.

**Research Findings (Corn):**
*   **2018 PTI Corn Study:**
    *   Conceal dual band applications of nitrogen **out-yielded traditional broadcast Weed-N-Feed (WNF) applications by +11.7 Bu/A**.
    *   Single band Conceal treatments also performed well, yielding within -3.2 Bu/A of dual Conceal and **+8.5 Bu/A over WNF**.
    *   Reducing WNF by 50% and complementing with a dual band Conceal treatment **added +9.3 Bu/A** over stand-alone 100% WNF.
    *   Multi-year data (2017-2018) showed Conceal dual band programs generating **+$10.56 to +$61.68/A in additional net returns** above a control (50% WNF + 50% Side-dress). The triple application of 25% WNF + 25% Conceal dual band + 50% Side-dress achieved the highest returns at **+$61.68/A**.
*   **2019 PTI Corn Study:**
    *   Conceal dual band nitrogen applications **out-yielded traditional WNF applications by +15.6 Bu/A**, with single band Conceal also outperforming WNF by +9.1 Bu/A.
    *   Planter-applied nitrogen with Conceal showed an average yield advantage of **+12.35 Bu/A** over WNF, equating to **+$45.32/A in additional revenue**.
    *   Conceal dual band applications **out-yielded single band applications by +5.2 Bu/A**, resulting in **+$18.94/A in additional net returns**.
    *   A triple split application (25% WNF + 25% Conceal dual band + 50% Side-dress) was the top nitrogen program for 2019, yielding **+$50.07 over the control**.
    *   Multi-year data (2017-2019) indicated Conceal at-plant nitrogen programs in the top three spots, netting **+$36.41 to +$57.81/A in additional returns** over the control.
    *   Conceal K-Fuse Potassium applications showed yield responses from +3.7 Bu/A to +11.6 Bu/A, with 5 Gal/A being economically optimum at **+$19.82/A net returns**.
    *   A split application of Kalibrate (2.5 Gal/A in FurrowJet and 2.5 Gal/A in Conceal) yielded **+11.7 Bu/A gains** and **+$10.97/A net returns**.
    *   Tank-mixing 3 Gal/A of ammonium thiosulfate (ATS) with UAN 32% via dual band Conceal provided **+7.4 Bu/A yield gains** and **+$21.85/A ROI**. Adding 1 qt. of 5% Boron further increased yields by +3.2 Bu/A and net returns by +$7.58/A.

**Research Findings (Soybeans):**
*   **2018 PTI Soybean Study:** Conceal dual band treatments of 14-12-4-6S at 10 gallons/acre provided **+4.9 Bu/A yield increases** and a **return on investment of +$25.20/A**.
*   **2019 PTI Soybean Study:**
    *   Conceal dual band treatments of NutriStart 12-12-4-6S (14-12-4-6S) showed yield gains from +8.9 Bu/A to +10.4 Bu/A. The economic optimum rate was 10 Gal/A, with an ROI of **+$84.96/A**.
    *   Conceal dual band K-Fuse applications increased yields by +2.7 to +4.4 Bu/A, with healthy returns ranging from **+$18.99/A to +$24.54/A**.
    *   Conceal dual band Marco Fertilizer's 0-0-29 potassium acetate at 4 Gal/A offered the highest yield response of **+8.2 Bu/A** and the highest net returns at **+$47.78/A**.

### vSet Seed Meters

vSet seed meters are engineered for accurate seed singulation without the need for continual adjustments.

**Key Features & Capabilities:**
*   **Accurate Singulation:** Utilizes a disk with holes (rather than cupped cells) and a floating singulator that automatically adjusts to the disk, ensuring a single seed per hole. This design allows for **99%+ singulation** in many crops.
*   **Maintenance-Free Operation:** Eliminates the constant adjustments required by traditional seed meters. Simply insert the correct crop kit, turn on the vacuum, and plant.
*   **Broad Crop Compatibility:** Works with **over 15 different crops** by using the appropriate crop kit.
*   **Industry Standard:** Chosen by **over 20 planter manufacturers worldwide** as their factory-installed meter.

**Benefits:**
*   **Picket Fence Stand:** Delivers perfect seed placement and ensures the correct number of seeds per foot, leading to a "picket fence stand".
*   **Reduced Seed Waste & Increased Profitability:** Prevents skips, doubles, and inaccurate populations that lead to lost yield and profitability.

**Research Findings:**
*   **2018 PTI Corn Study:** 95% seed singulation (compared to a 99.5% control) resulted in **-10.4 Bu/A yield losses** and **-$36.49/A economic losses**. This equates to a **-2.1 Bu/A loss for each percentage of singulation lost**.
*   **2019 PTI Corn Study:** 95% seed singulation (compared to a 99.5% control) resulted in **-12.7 Bu/A yield losses** and **-$46.79/A economic losses**. This equates to a **-2.8 Bu/A loss for each percentage of singulation lost**.
*   **2019 PTI Soybean Study:** Using a 56-hole soybean disk to singulate soybeans resulted in **+1.2 Bu/A yield increases** over an 80-hole disk, leading to an economic advantage of **+$10.42/A**.

### DeltaForce

DeltaForce is an automated, row-by-row downforce control system designed to optimize planting depth and plant growth.

**Key Features & Capabilities:**
*   **Automated Row-by-Row Adjustment:** Replaces traditional springs or air bags with hydraulic cylinders, allowing it to automatically measure and adjust downforce for each row individually.
*   **Real-Time Adaptability:** Adjusts independently as each row encounters varying field conditions such as wheel tracks, old road beds, clay knobs, or headlands.
*   **Prevents Compaction & Shallow Planting:** Balances downforce to prevent issues like late emergence from too little force (shallow planting in dry soil) or restricted root growth from too much force (furrow smearing and sidewall compaction). Can even lift weight off the row unit if it's too heavy.

**Benefits:**
*   **Optimal Yields:** Ensures each seed is planted at the correct depth in soil free from compaction, fostering uniform germination, optimum growth, and maximum yield.
*   **Eliminates Guesswork:** Removes the need for manual guesswork in setting downforce, leading to improved emergence and yield.

**Research Findings (Corn):**
*   **2018 PTI Corn Study:**
    *   Too light downforce resulted in the largest losses at **-12.8 Bu/A**, while excess downforce caused **-4.8 Bu/A losses**.
    *   DeltaForce automated downforce increased revenue by **+$16.87/A** compared to heavy settings and **+$44.95/A** compared to light settings, with average overall gains of **+$30.91/A**.
*   **2019 PTI Corn Study:**
    *   Too light downforce resulted in **-20.7 Bu/A losses**, while excess downforce caused **-3.5 Bu/A losses**.
    *   In difficult cloddy conditions, proper downforce from DeltaForce ensured row units maintained ground contact, leading to proper planting depth and seed placement into adequate moisture.

**Research Findings (Soybeans):**
*   **2019 PTI Soybean Study:**
    *   In a study comparing automatic downforce settings at 1.5” depth, the **Standard Setting outperformed both Light Automatic and Heavy Automatic settings by 1.7 to 1.8 Bu/A**.
    *   Using incorrect downforce targets (Light or Heavy) resulted in economic losses of **-$14.76 to -$15.62/A**.
    *   At various planting depths (1” to 2.5”), the Standard automatic mode achieved the highest soybean yields at **+67.0 Bu/A** (at 2" depth). Heavy automatic mode realized the lowest yields at 64.8 Bu/A.

### FurrowForce

FurrowForce is a two-stage closing system engineered to remove air pockets and firm soil, optimizing seed germination and emergence. It can be operated mechanically or automated with the 20|20 monitor.

**Key Features & Capabilities:**
*   **Two-Stage Closing Process:**
    *   **Stage 1:** Notched wheels aggressively close the seed furrow from the bottom to the top, eliminating air pockets.
    *   **Stage 2:** Stitch wheels carry weight to firm the soil over the seed, retaining moisture for quick and consistent germination. These wheels also set the depth for the first stage's cut, ensuring good seed-to-soil contact.
*   **Automated Adjustment (with 20|20 monitor):** Allows the operator to set the desired firming force from the cab. A load cell measures the weight on the second-stage stitch wheels, and FurrowForce automatically adjusts the force, row by row, to overcome field variability.
*   **Sidewall Fracture:** The system's design can assist in closing the furrow by lifting and fracturing sidewall compaction or smearing caused by disc openers.

**Benefits:**
*   **Consistent Germination:** Adapts to planting conditions to ensure optimal germination.
*   **Confidence in Settings:** Eliminates guesswork, providing confidence that closing system pressure settings are correct. Farmers often note, "I can't find the furrow," after using it, indicating effective closing.

**Research Findings (Corn):**
*   **2019 PTI Corn Study:**
    *   In **no-till environments**, the FurrowForce automated sensing and control system significantly out-yielded non-sensing/control systems by **+5.2 to +10.2 Bu/A**, leading to additional returns of **+$19.08 to +$37.43/A**.
    *   In **vertical-till environments**, FurrowForce also showed positive yield gains of **+5.1 to +6.8 Bu/A** over non-sensing/control systems, equating to **+$18.72 to +$24.96/A** in additional returns.
    *   In **strip-till environments**, the dual Yetter Poly Twister closing system proved highest yields, with a +0.7 Bu/A advantage over FurrowForce.
    *   Averaged over all tillage environments, FurrowForce showed yield gains of **+4.1 to +6.8 Bu/A** over all other closing systems, while non-sensing closing wheels suffered average economic losses of **-$20.10/A** compared to FurrowForce.

**Research Findings (Soybeans):**
*   **2019 PTI Soybean Study:**
    *   In **no-till environments**, FurrowForce showed yield gains of **+2.0 to +3.5 Bu/A** over non-sensing/control systems, equating to additional returns of **+$17.36 to +$30.38/A**.
    *   In **vertical-till environments**, FurrowForce showed yield gains of **+1.6 to +3.8 Bu/A** over non-sensing/control systems, equating to additional returns of **+$13.89 to +$32.98/A**.
    *   In **strip-till environments**, FurrowForce showed yield gains of **+0.2 to +3.3 Bu/A** over non-sensing/control systems, equating to additional returns of **+$1.74 to +$28.64/A**.
    *   In **conventional-till environments**, FurrowForce showed yield gains of **+0.3 to +2.7 Bu/A** over non-sensing/control systems, equating to additional returns of **+$2.60 to +$23.44/A**.
    *   Overall, FurrowForce achieved average gains of **+$20.26/A** over all non-sensing closing systems.

### FurrowJet

FurrowJet is a planter fertilizer attachment that strategically places nutrients on and near the seed at planting.

**Key Features & Capabilities:**
*   **Optimal Starter Placement:** Firms the seed in the trench while simultaneously placing bands of fertilizer on and near the seed.
*   **Dual-Band Application:** Rides in the furrow just above the seed. Its wings angle downward to cut into the sidewall, placing a dual band of fertilizer **3/4” on each side of the seed**.
*   **Early Nutrient Access:** This placement ensures early roots, including crown roots, have immediate and continuous access to essential nutrients at the perfect time.

**Benefits:**
*   **Reduced Risk, Increased Yield:** Allows for increased rates of common starter fertilizers to be applied safely, avoiding seed burn risk, which reduces risk and increases yield.
*   **Addresses In-Season Hunger:** Prevents plants from being "hungry" by providing nutrients when they are needed most.
*   **Sidewall Compaction Management:** The wing design can help lift and fracture sidewall compaction or smearing caused by disc openers, improving seed trench closing.

**Research Findings (Corn):**
*   **2018 PTI Corn Study:**
    *   FurrowJet treatments of Marco QuickGrow LTE **averaged +9.1 Bu/A gains**, with **8 gal/A being economically optimum (+$11.53/A ROI)**.
    *   Nachurs imPulse (10-18-4) via FurrowJet at 2 gal/A was **economically optimum (+$18.47/A ROI)** with **+8.3 Bu/A yield gains**.
    *   Ethos XB (insecticide/fungicide) via FurrowJet showed **+8.1 Bu/A gains** and **+$8.03/A ROI**.
    *   Capture LFR (insecticide) via FurrowJet at 4 oz/A showed **+5.8 Bu/A gains** and **+$11.68/A ROI**.
    *   FurrowJet wing-only applications **out-yielded center treatments by +5.4 Bu/A** with an average net return advantage of **+$9.52/A**. A combined center + wing (relay application) further increased yields by **+9.4 Bu/A** with additional net returns of **+$20.59/A**.
    *   FurrowJet placement of 10-34-0 provided **+$10.55/A net returns**, outperforming Conceal placement of the same product.
*   **2019 PTI Corn Study:**
    *   Marco QuickGrow LTE (6-20-4-.25Zn-2.7S) via FurrowJet showed 100% positive yield increases. **14 Gal/A offered the highest yield (+8.11 Bu/A)**, and **10 Gal/A was economically optimum (+$24.73/A ROI)**.
    *   Nachurs imPulse (10-18-4) via FurrowJet: **3X3 Gal/A application was agronomic and economic optimum (+$51.57/A ROI, +11.6 Bu/A yield gain)**.
    *   Helena Nucleus O-Phos (8-24-0) via FurrowJet: O-Phos/Zinc treatments averaged **+7.5 Bu/A yield responses** and economic gains of **+$23.34 to +$25.20/A**.
    *   Helena Nucleus HP (8-24-4) via FurrowJet: The 3 Gal/A rate with an extra quart of zinc achieved the highest returns of **+$23.74/A** with **+6.4 Bu/A yield**.
    *   FurrowJet sidewall study showed **+2.5 Bu/A advantages** in 2019.

**Research Findings (Soybeans):**
*   **2018 PTI Soybean Study:**
    *   SoyShot (0-10-10-0.1Zn) via FurrowJet center at 1-2 Gal/A resulted in **+2.6 Bu/A gains** and **+$11.92/A net returns**.
    *   NutriStart (7-22-5-.25Zn) via FurrowJet wings at 8 Gal/A yielded **+5.5 Bu/A** and **+$26.18/A ROI**.
    *   Nachurs in-furrow and foliar treatments (playmaKer, CropMax, Triple Option, Rhizo-Link) increased soybean yield by **+6.2 Bu/A**, translating to **+$33.76/A ROI**.
*   **2019 PTI Soybean Study:**
    *   Marco QuickGrow LTE (6-20-4-.25Zn-2.7S) via FurrowJet wings: **6 Gal/A was agronomic and economic optimum (+$22.40/A ROI, +4.9 Bu/A yield advantage)**.
    *   AgroLiquid in-furrow and foliar nutrition (Pro-Germinator, Sure-K, Micro 500, C-Tech, Ferti-Rain, Boron, Manganese) via FurrowJet: showed **+7.2 Bu/A average yield** and **+$19.93/A ROI** in non-irrigated environments. In irrigated environments, yields increased by **+18.8 Bu/A** with **+$39.69/A ROI**.

### ReClaim

ReClaim is a recirculation system for sprayers that solves challenges related to nozzle bar section priming and cleanout.

**Key Features & Capabilities:**
*   **Recirculation System:** Adds a new flow path from the ends of each nozzle bar section back to the tank.
*   **Automated Priming:** Starts product flow through the system with a simple switch flip, indicating when product has recirculated back to the tank.
*   **Enhanced Cleanout:** Allows clean water from a dedicated tank (if available) to circulate through all nozzle bar sections back to the tank. Alternatively, compressed air can force liquid through the return path for full cleanout.
*   **Eliminates Dead Zones:** Designed to eliminate dead zones at end caps where sediment typically accumulates.

**Benefits:**
*   **Waste Reduction:** Eliminates wasteful and unsafe priming by ensuring correct product concentration at every nozzle before spraying.
*   **Time & Cost Savings:** Saves time and product by preventing the need to spray out excess product at the ditch.
*   **Environmental Stewardship:** Reduces the dumping of costly and potentially hazardous chemicals onto the ground.
*   **Improved Safety:** Contributes to safer operations by reducing exposure to chemicals.
*   **Retrofit Compatibility:** Can be retrofitted onto existing self-propelled or pull-type sprayers, supporting traditional, electric, or PWM nozzles.

### Precision Planting Fertility System (General Product Overview)

The Precision Planting Fertility System provides superior placement and control products to ensure crops receive essential nutrients precisely when and where they are needed.

**Key Concepts:**
*   **Strategic Placement:** Shifting a portion of fall-applied fertilizer to a planter-applied banded application can **reduce the total amount of needed fertility by up to 30%**. This places nutrients under the soil surface, ensuring they are available to plants when needed, unlike inefficient broadcast applications that expose nutrients to surface volatilization.
*   **Rate Control:** Addressing up to 30% variability in current liquid control systems is critical for maximizing fertility dollars.

**Placement Products:**
*   **FurrowJet®:** Firms the seed in the trench while placing bands of fertilizer on and near the seed, allowing early roots to access essential nutrients at the perfect time.
*   **Conceal®:** A planter fertility attachment that is cleanly tucked into the row-unit and places nutrition in the soil **3 inches away from the seed**.

**Control Products:**
*   **FlowSense™:** Allows you to measure the rate of fertilizer being applied on each row of your planter. This makes you aware of any row-to-row variability, ensuring confidence in your application rate.
*   **EMHD®:** Controls liquid application rates using an electromagnetic flow meter, expanding options for a wider range of liquid products with high accuracy.
*   **Pump Stack:** Simplifies the pairing of application technology with fertility rates. It's designed to work with Precision Planting’s Fertility System or other rate controllers, making it easy to design a liquid fertility system.

### SmartFirmer

SmartFirmer is a seed firmer-sensor that provides real-time information about the seed's environment directly in the furrow.

**Key Features & Capabilities:**
*   **Real-Time In-Furrow Sensing:** Measures three critical aspects of the seed environment:
    *   **Seed-Available Moisture:** Measures the amount of moisture available to the seed and displays it in the cab, allowing for depth adjustments to ensure consistent germination. Less than 30% furrow moisture indicates a need for adjustment.
    *   **Soil Temperature:** Measures soil temperature as planting occurs, informing decisions about optimal planting times (e.g., early morning or after sunset) to prevent delayed emergence from chilled seeds.
    *   **Residue in Seed Furrow:** Measures residue levels, enabling adjustments to row cleaners to avoid residue contact with the seed, which can cause late germination.
*   **20|20 Monitor Integration:** All measured information is displayed on the 20|20 monitor in the cab.

**Benefits:**
*   **Complete Environmental Picture:** Provides a more complete picture of whether the depth setting is correct than simply digging a few seeds.
*   **Consistent Crop Stand:** Enables informed decisions to place seeds in the ideal environment for fast, uniform germination, leading to maximized yields.
*   **Optimized Planting Depth:** Helps farmers choose the right planting depth as soil conditions change.

**Research Findings (Corn):**
*   **2018 PTI Corn Study:**
    *   Optimum planting depth was found to be 2 inches. Yields decreased significantly when planting shallower (-3.7 to -7.8 Bu/A) or deeper than 2 inches (-6.9 to -15.3 Bu/A).
    *   Economic losses from incorrect planting depths ranged from **-$12.95 to -$53.55/A**.
    *   Research indicates that soil moisture levels near **30%** are adequate for seed imbibition and emergence. Values below 30% may require deeper planting.
*   **2019 PTI Corn Study:**
    *   Shallow planting depths resulted in **-7.8 Bu/A yield losses** by placing seed into dry soil.
    *   **3-inch planting depths were the only ones that consistently achieved 30% or more furrow moisture**.
    *   Planting deeper to achieve at least 30% furrow moisture could increase revenue by **+$46.60/A**.
